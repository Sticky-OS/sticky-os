{{NAME}} 1.9: a daily Sudoku for the fridge door

{{NAME}} 1.9 adds a new daily puzzle, Sudoku. It sits under Daily in the Games folder, next to Daily maths: a new puzzle every day, made on the board itself from the date, with no server and no account, and the same puzzle on every board running {{NAME}} 1.9.

A puzzle a day

The Daily tile in the Games folder now opens the puzzles of the day: Daily maths, as before, and Sudoku. Each day brings a new Sudoku at three levels: Easy, with 36 to 40 numbers given, Medium with 30 to 34, and Hard with 26 to 29. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

The board makes the puzzle itself, from the date. It needs no server and no account, nothing is sent anywhere, and every board running the same version of {{NAME}} has the same puzzle that day: one to compare over breakfast. Each puzzle keeps its own streak of days in a row.

Why e-paper suits it

Sudoku is a game of looking, not of speed: a grid that sits still, a number now and then. That is what e-paper does well. The puzzle stays on the screen, sharp as print, and is there to pick up again each time you pass the fridge. Today’s grid, your notes and your time are kept even when the board restarts or sleeps on battery; a new day starts afresh.

How to play

Fill the grid so that every row, every column and every 3 × 3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9 once each. Tap Daily, then Sudoku, and pick a level. Tap a square, then a number; the given numbers are bold and can’t change. Pencil puts numbers in small, as notes, Erase clears a square and Undo takes back a step. A number that clashes with another in its row, column or box is struck through, never refused.

The clock counts while the puzzle is on screen. Fill the last square and the board shows your time, a new best if it is one, your streak (days in a row with a sudoku solved, at any level) and Come back tomorrow for a new one. How to play the games has every step, on the board’s own screens.

Get it

{{NAME}} 1.9 is on GitHub Releases. On a board running {{NAME}}, open Settings → Software update, tap Check now, then Install. To install it the first time, see Install. Sudoku is under Daily in the Games folder.
