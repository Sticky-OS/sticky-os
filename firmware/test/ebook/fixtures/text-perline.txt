PART ONE

Chapter 1: The Ink
It was winter on the river, and the ink in the lantern room had gone thick and slow, so nobody read at night any more, not even the old ferryman.
The paper boats stayed still along the quay, waiting for spring, and the café on the corner kept its shutters down from November until the thaw.
