CHAPTER I.
Winter

It was winter on the river, and the ink in the lantern room had gone
thick and slow. Nobody read at night any more; the café was shut and
the paper boats stayed _very_ still along the quay, waiting for spring.

Chapter one of the old book was the best part, she said, and closed it.

    How quiet the river,
        How dark the ink,
    How slow the winter
        Before you think.

CHAPTER II.

The Boat

A paper boat is a small thing, but it carries a word from one bank to
the other if the wind is kind and the reader patient enough to wait.
