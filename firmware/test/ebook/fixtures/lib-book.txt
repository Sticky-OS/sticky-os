A Walk to the Mill

Written for the library's test; no rights reserved.

Chapter 1

The path to the mill ran along the stream, and in the first week of October it was covered with leaves the colour of toast. Ana walked it every morning with a bag of bread ends for the ducks, who knew her step and came up the bank before she had reached the bridge.

That morning there was a hedgehog on the bridge. It sat in the middle of the planks, quite still, as if it had forgotten which side it had been going to. Ana stopped. The ducks, who had no patience for hedgehogs, went back to the water.

"Well," she said, "one of us will have to decide."

Chapter 2

The miller had seen stranger things than a girl carrying a hedgehog in a bread bag, but not many, and not before breakfast. He put down his cup, looked into the bag, and looked at Ana.

"It was on the bridge," she said. "It didn't know which way."

"They never do," said the miller. He found a crate, and some straw, and an apple cut in four. By the time the wheel had turned a hundred times the hedgehog had eaten three of the quarters and gone to sleep on the last.
